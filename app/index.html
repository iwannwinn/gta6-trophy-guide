The road to the platinum in six stages, built from the official descriptions: story first, side strands before the finale, then activities, collectibles, combat and crime, and the money mop-up. Each stage lists its trophies in the order to earn them.

Fifteen trophies can be missed — or are safest done early. Six are named story missions that can only be earned while the mission is live, unless the game lets you replay missions (not yet confirmed for this game). Six are side-character strands and stranger strands that may lock after the final story mission. Three are one-time choices or events. The game launched yesterday and no walkthrough confirms missability yet, so this page flags them from the official descriptions and says plainly where the facts run out.

What to chase next, in order: missable trophies first, then the story in order, then quick single-action trophies, then the long counters. Earned trophies drop out of the list as you tick them off.
